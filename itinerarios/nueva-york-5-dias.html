🇺🇸 New York in 5 days

Midtown views, Central Park, the High Line, Brooklyn at sunset and a Broadway show. Budget excluding flights and accommodation.

5 días26 planes~650 €/personaPensado para 4
Ábrelo en YOKA

Gratis en iPhone (beta en Android). En la app, búscalo en Explorar → Itinerarios: «New York in 5 days». Cópialo a tu viaje, votad los planes y repartid los gastos.

Día 1 · Midtown

  1. 09:00

    Grand Central Terminal

    New York Plan

    Look up at the ceiling and try the whispering gallery downstairs.

  2. 10:30

    MoMA

    New York Museo

    Book a time slot

  3. 13:30

    Rockefeller Center

    New York Plan

    Ice rink in winter, plaza and shops all year.

  4. 17:30

    Top of the Rock

    New York Plan

    Book sunset slot

  5. 20:00

    Broadway show (TKTS)

    New York Noche

    Same-day discount tickets at the red TKTS steps.

  6. 22:30

    Times Square

    New York Plan

    Only at night, and only for a few minutes.

Día 2 · Central Park & museums

  1. 09:00

    Levain Bakery

    New York Comer

    Huge, gooey cookies. Share one, they're massive.

  2. 10:00

    Central Park · Bethesda Terrace

    New York Naturaleza

    Rent a bike and do the full loop. Autumn colors are unreal.

  3. 13:00

    The Met

    New York Museo

    Don't miss the Temple of Dendur and the rooftop in summer.

  4. 17:00

    Roosevelt Island Tram

    New York Plan

    Cable car over the East River for the price of a subway ride.

Día 3 · West Side

  1. 10:00

    Little Island

    New York Naturaleza

    Floating park on tulip-shaped pillars. Free.

  2. 10:45

    Chelsea Market

    New York Comer

    Tacos, lobster rolls and doughnuts under one roof.

  3. 12:00

    The High Line

    New York Naturaleza

    South to north

  4. 13:30

    Edge · Hudson Yards

    New York Plan

    Glass floor sticking out of the 100th floor.

  5. 17:00

    Greenwich Village · Washington Square

    New York Plan

    Street musicians under the arch. Perfect for wandering.

  6. 19:30

    Joe's Pizza

    New York Comer

    The classic New York slice. Fold it in half.

Día 4 · Downtown & Brooklyn

  1. 09:00

    Staten Island Ferry

    New York Plan

    Free ride with great Statue of Liberty views. Stand on the right.

  2. 11:00

    9/11 Memorial & Museum

    New York Museo

    The pools are free. The museum is moving; give it 2 hours.

  3. 13:30

    Katz's Delicatessen

    New York Comer

    Pastrami on rye. Don't lose your ticket at the door.

  4. 15:30

    Brooklyn Bridge

    New York Plan

    Walk it from Manhattan to Brooklyn, with the skyline behind you.

  5. 16:30

    DUMBO

    New York Plan

    The famous photo of the bridge from Washington Street.

  6. 18:00

    Brooklyn Bridge Park

    New York Naturaleza

    Watch the sun set behind Manhattan.

Día 5 · SoHo & Williamsburg

  1. 10:00

    SoHo

    New York Compras

    Cast-iron buildings and every shop you can think of.

  2. 12:00

    Chinatown & Little Italy

    New York Plan

    Dumplings in Chinatown, cannoli in Little Italy.

  3. 15:00

    Williamsburg

    New York Compras

    Vintage shops, coffee and rooftop bars.

  4. 21:00

    Empire State Building

    New York Plan

    Short lines at night
